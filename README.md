A modern, professional tournament management system for University of Eldoret students.

## ✨ Features

- **Player Registration** - Kenyan phone validation, year of study selection
- **KES 33 Entry Fee** - Simulated M-Pesa payment system
- **WhatsApp Integration** - Group link unlocks after payment
- **UCL Tournament Format** - Group stage → Knockout rounds → Final
- **Live Countdown** - 2-week timer to tournament start
- **Admin Dashboard** - Manage fixtures, results, player status
- **300 Player Limit** - Real-time slot counter
- **18-Hour Rule** - Automatic disqualification for missed matches
- **Prize Pool** - Winner takes 90% of collected fees

## 🏆 Tournament Structure

- **Format**: UEFA Champions League style
- **Groups**: Multiple groups (depending on registrations)
- **Knockout**: Round of 16 → Quarter Finals → Semi Finals → Grand Final
- **Prize**: 90% of total entry fees to champion

## 🔐 Admin Access

- **Password**: `UoE2025!`

## 📱 How to Participate

1. **Register** - Fill in your details (name, Kenyan phone, year of study)
2. **Pay KES 33** - Use the simulated M-Pesa payment
3. **Join WhatsApp** - Group link unlocks automatically after payment
4. **Play Matches** - Follow fixtures and schedule
5. **Win Prize** - Champion takes 90% of prize pool

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- HTML5

- LocalStorage (Client-side data persistence)

## 📋 Rules & Regulations

1. **Eligibility**: University of Eldoret students only
2. **Entry Fee**: KES 33 (non-refundable after tournament starts)
3. **Match Schedule**: Must play within 18 hours of scheduled time
4. **Disqualification**: Missing matches within 18-hour window results in automatic DQ
5. **Fair Play**: Cheating results in immediate ban
6. **Legal**: This is a student-organized event, not affiliated with KONAMI or University Board

## 💰 Prize Distribution

- **1st Place**: 90% of total entry fees
- **2nd Place**: Bragging rights & recognition
- **10%**: Tournament operations & organization
